## 1. What RaHel is
RaHel is a Wear OS watch app (package `xyz.targapps.rahel`) that reads heart rate and steps from your watch, applies wellness assessment rules and, if you link your watch to an account, syncs that data to a web dashboard. RaHel is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition, and it does not replace the advice of a health professional.

## 2. Data we collect
**Health and activity data (watch sensors, with your permission):**
- Heart rate, measured by the watch.
- Daily steps and activity recognition, measured by the watch.

**Estimates (not measurements):** resting heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), sleep and stress are estimates that our server calculates from your heart rate and step history. They are labeled as estimated and are not direct measurements. RaHel does not read blood oxygen (SpO2) or skin temperature.

**Information you enter:** biological sex, weight, height, the body mass index (BMI) calculated from them, and symptoms you report yourself in the app.

**Generated by the app:** wellness assessments produced by the app's rules (level, summary, possible conditions and recommended actions).

**Linking and technical data:** member/profile identifier (crewMemberId), a device token issued when you link the watch (stored encrypted on the device), a sync batch identifier, timestamps, and a technical inventory of the sensors available on the watch. We do not collect location, contacts, photos, microphone audio or advertising identifiers.

## 3. How we use the data
- Calculate wellness assessments and alerts.
- Show your history and trends on the web dashboard when you link your watch.
- Operate, maintain and improve the security and stability of the service.

We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with third parties for commercial purposes. The only exception is the optional AI analysis described in section 5A, which happens only if you request it and consent to it.

## 4. Where the data is stored
- **On the watch:** profile, preferences and a local queue (Room database) of readings waiting to be sent. The device token is stored encrypted.
- **On our server** (`rahel.hermesbackend.xyz`, HTTPS API), hosted by [hosting provider] in [country], only if you link the watch to an account. Linking uses a one-time code that you generate for your account, and data is sent over HTTPS to that account. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS).
- **Demo mode** does not send anything to the server.
- The app does not use Android cloud backups (`allowBackup=false`).

## 5. Who we share it with
Only with infrastructure providers that act as data processors (server hosting: [hosting provider]) and where required by law. There are no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the app. If (and only if) you run the optional AI analysis, TypeSafe AI acts as a data processor on the terms in section 5A.

## 5A. Optional AI analysis (TypeSafe AI)
On the web dashboard, the "Analysis" page lets you generate a wellness report on demand using TypeSafe AI models (the Jev model). It is **entirely optional**: the app and the dashboard work the same without it.

- **Who:** TypeSafe AI ([provider address / country]) acts as a **data processor** on behalf of RaHel, solely to return the answers for the report. [State the signed contract/DPA and whether TypeSafe AI retains or trains on the data submitted, according to its current terms.]
- **Legal basis:** your **explicit consent, given on every run** through a checkbox shown before the "Run analysis" button. Nothing is sent without it. You can withdraw consent at any time by not using the feature.
- **What is sent:** an **aggregated summary** of the period you choose (24 hours, 7 or 30 days): heart rate statistics by time of day, average steps, relative daily trends ("2 days ago"), episode counts, data coverage and the estimates our server already calculates (resting heart rate, HRV, sleep, stress), together with fixed wellness questions.
- **What is NOT sent:** your email, name, account, watch or profile identifiers, tokens, second-by-second readings, exact dates and times, or location. The access key for TypeSafe AI lives only on our server.
- **Frequency:** only when you press "Run analysis"; never in the background or in real time. There is a limit on runs per hour.
- **What we store:** the report (answers and plain-language readings), the date, the period, the date of your consent and a hash of the summary that was sent. **We do not store the submitted summary or the content of requests in logs**; server logs contain only metadata (duration, number of questions, errors).
- **Retention and deletion:** reports are kept in our database until you delete them from the page itself ("Delete") or through `DELETE /api/analysis/runs/:id`, or until you delete your account. Data sent to TypeSafe AI is governed by its policy: [link to TypeSafe AI policy / DPA].
- **Nature of the result:** these are AI-generated wellness assessments based on estimates; they are not a diagnosis and not a medical device.

## 6. Retention

## 8. Permissions the app requests and why
| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Body sensors (BODY_SENSORS / health.READ_HEART_RATE) | Read heart rate. |
| Background health data (health.READ_HEALTH_DATA_IN_BACKGROUND) | Keep reading heart rate while the screen is off. |
| Activity recognition (ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION) | Count steps and detect activity. |
| Health foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_HEALTH) | Keep monitoring running while the watch is idle. The service runs only after you tap "Start monitoring" and stops when you tap "Stop monitoring". |
| Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) | Show wellness alerts and the active monitoring notification. |
| Internet / network state | Sync with the web dashboard when the watch is linked. |
| High sampling rate sensors, WAKE_LOCK | Accurate readings and reliable sync. |
